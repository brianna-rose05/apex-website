About

Built by someone who quoted countertops by hand.

Apex started at the sales desk of a stone fabrication shop, not in a boardroom. It exists to give that time back.

OUR MISSION

Every countertop quote should take minutes, not days. So fabricators spend their time selling and building, and homeowners get a real number while they're still excited about their kitchen.

THE STORY

From the sales desk to every shop.

Every quote meant measuring, sketching, doing the square-foot math, looking up stone prices in one spreadsheet and edge rates in another, then waiting on a customer who had already called twice.

So we built the tool we wished we had: a guided flow that lets the homeowner do the measuring, calculates the area of any shape exactly, and keeps the shop's pricing locked on the server.
